PlantDoctor — Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2, 2026

PlantDoctor (“we”, “our”, or “us”) is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights as a user.

1. Data We Collect

Photos. When you scan a plant, your photo is uploaded to Firebase Storage and sent to our plant identification and diagnosis services for processing. Photos are stored under your account and deleted when you delete your account.

Location. If you grant location permission, your approximate GPS coordinates are used solely to fetch local weather data for personalised care advice. Coordinates are not stored on our servers — they are used only at the moment of the request.

Account data. You may use the app as a guest (no sign-in required) or sign in with Google, Apple, or email. Guest sessions are identified by an anonymous ID only. If you sign in, we store your email address and display name via Firebase Authentication.

Scan history. Your plant scan results — species name, health diagnosis, and care advice — are stored in your account in Firebase Firestore so you can access them across sessions and devices.

2. Third-Party Services

PlantDoctor uses the following third-party services to deliver its features:

  • Firebase (Google) — authentication, database, file storage, and analytics. Privacy policy
  • Pl@ntNet API — plant species identification. Your photo is sent to Pl@ntNet for processing. Privacy policy
  • OpenWeatherMap — local weather data. Only your approximate coordinates are sent; no account data is shared. Privacy policy
  • Anthropic Claude — AI-powered plant health diagnosis. Your photo and identified species name are sent to Anthropic for analysis. Privacy policy

3. How We Use Your Data

  • To identify plant species and diagnose health issues
  • To personalise care advice using local weather conditions
  • To store and display your scan history across devices
  • To send watering reminders and care alerts (only if you enable notifications)

We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

4. Data Retention & Deletion

Your scan history and account data are retained until you request deletion. To request deletion of all your data, contact us at squid55644@gmail.com with the subject line “Delete my data”. We will permanently remove all your data within 30 days.

5. Children’s Privacy

PlantDoctor is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can delete it.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top reflects when changes were last made. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

7. Contact

Questions about this privacy policy or your data? Email us at squid55644@gmail.com.
